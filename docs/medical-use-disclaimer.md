# Medical and Research-Use Disclaimer

`wound3d` is research and development software for three-dimensional reconstruction and analysis of wound imagery.

It is **not** a medical device, has not been clinically validated, and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate,
or prevent any disease or condition. It must not be used as the sole basis for clinical decisions.

Users are responsible for obtaining required consent and ethics approvals; complying with privacy, data-protection, and
institutional requirements; validating outputs for their own hardware and acquisition protocol; and ensuring protected
health information, identifiable videos, images, masks, reconstructions, and metadata are never committed or shared
without appropriate authorization.

The maintainers provide no guarantee that reconstructed geometry, segmentation, scaling, or measurements are accurate for
any clinical or scientific purpose.
